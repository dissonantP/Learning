The archive that executes

Today’s two focuses unexpectedly meet on the same distinction: storing information versus making it operational. A verifier turns a specification into an executable judgment; Sedira and Ono turn historical or linguistic material into instructions that can be re-instantiated. The interesting object is not the stored artifact but the machinery that makes it act again.
