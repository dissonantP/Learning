Conceptual art dossier

The body as a hostile interface

Seven works where spectatorship, institutions and politics are forced through bodies rather than represented at a safe distance.

The connection across these works is not “the body” in a generic sense. It is forced participation: bodies are made to carry sponsorship, racism, institutional memory, spectatorship, collective action or sexual power when language alone would let the viewer stay comfortable.
