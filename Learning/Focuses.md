### AI agent infrastructure
Priority: High

Focus on:
- AI agent orchestration architectures
- MCP-style tool use and protocol changes
- verifier / critic / evaluator loops
- cron, webhook, and event-driven agent automation
- ephemeral coding environments and agent sandboxes

### Conceptual art
Priority: High

Explore conceptual art broadly, with a strong preference for work that is provocative, controversial, socially critical, transgressive, confrontational, politically charged, taboo-breaking, or otherwise difficult rather than safe or conventional survey material.

Presentation requirements:
- Make conceptual-art coverage highly visual and embed images directly in the HTML edition.
- Prefer many examples over a deep dive into a single artwork. A report should normally surface multiple distinct works, artists, interventions, exhibitions, or projects when enough strong material is available.
- Give each example only a concise synopsis, up to one short paragraph, unless a particular work genuinely warrants more context.
- Show the work itself whenever a usable image is available; prefer reliable museum, archive, artist, gallery, or Wikimedia sources, and include attribution/source links with captions where practical.
- Do not sanitize the selection merely because a work is offensive, disturbing, politically contentious, sexually explicit, sacrilegious, or otherwise controversial. Contextualize why the work mattered and what it was engaging with.
- Favor surprising connections, lesser-known examples, historical precedents, and contemporary work rather than repeatedly returning to the same canonical pieces.
